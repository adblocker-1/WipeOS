WipeOS - company logos
======================

Put your company logo into this folder. It is printed in the letterhead
(top left) of every erasure report.

- Name it company-logo.png (or company-logo.jpg) and it is used
  automatically - or choose any image in this folder in WipeOS under
  "Your company".
- Format: PNG (transparent background recommended) or JPG
- Max. file size: 5 MB
- Max. size: 4000 x 4000 pixels (min. 16 x 16)
- Recommended: landscape logo, at least 600 px wide, e.g. 1200 x 300 px
- On the report the logo is fitted into 70 x 18 mm, keeping its aspect
  ratio. Transparent borders are trimmed automatically.
