WIPEOS
======

Starting
--------
Plug in the stick and boot the computer from USB (boot menu, usually F12,
F9, F11 or Esc). In the boot menu choose "WipeOS" (starts automatically).
The user interface appears after a few seconds. Use a mouse, touchscreen or
keyboard (Tab = next field, Enter = continue).

Workflow
--------
1. Create job: create a new customer or pick an existing one, choose the
   technician and enter a system name for the device.
   Serial number, manufacturer and model are read from the BIOS.
2. Select drives (the WipeOS stick itself is locked).
3. Choose a method ("PRNG Stream" is recommended) and confirm.
4. Erasing - do not switch the device off.
5. Finished: the erasure report (PDF) is saved on this stick in
   wipeos\reports, next to the nwipe log.

Files on this stick
-------------------
All your data is in the "wipeos" folder:

wipeos\config\     Settings and lists (plain text, editable on a PC)
  company.txt        Your company: name, address, phone/email, logo
                     (also editable in WipeOS under "Your company")
  customers.csv      Customer list (semicolon separated, opens in
                     Excel/LibreOffice)
  technicians.csv    Technicians (ID;Name)
  job_counter.txt    Counter for job numbers
wipeos\logos\      Company logos (PNG / JPG)
wipeos\reports\    Erasure reports (PDF) and nwipe logs

Sticks from WipeOS 1.1 are converted automatically on the first start
(old files are moved into these folders).

Company logo
------------
Copy your logo into wipeos\logos on any PC. Name it company-logo.png
(or .jpg) and it is used automatically, or pick any PNG/JPG in that
folder in WipeOS under "Your company".
- Format: PNG (transparent background recommended) or JPG
- Max. file size 5 MB, max. 4000 x 4000 pixels
- Recommended: landscape logo, at least 600 px wide, e.g. 1200 x 300 px
- On the report the logo is fitted into 70 x 18 mm (top left), keeping
  its aspect ratio; transparent borders are trimmed automatically.

Notes
-----
- Method: overwriting with nwipe followed by verification.
  Random data comes from ChaCha20 (cryptographically secure).
- Hardware erase (ATA Secure Erase / NVMe Sanitize) is available in the
  classic nwipe interface: "Classic nwipe" button in step 1 or the boot
  menu entry "WipeOS (classic nwipe interface)".
- Keyboard layout: toggle US/DE in the sidebar of step 1, or add
  "wipeos_kbd=de" to the linux line in the boot menu (press "e").
- Console: Ctrl+Alt+F2.

WipeOS is based on ShredOS (github.com/PartialVolume/shredos.x86_64)
and nwipe (github.com/martijnvanbrummelen/nwipe), both GPL-2.0.
